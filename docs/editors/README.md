# Editor help

This help is for anyone who writes on a cairn site through the editor at `/admin`. Each page covers one job, and the pages below are in the order most people need them. If you opened this help because something went wrong, start with [When something goes wrong](./when-something-goes-wrong.md).

1. [Welcome to the editor](./welcome.md) gets you signed in and shows what appears afterward.
2. [Write in the editor](./write-in-the-editor.md) takes you from a blank entry to finished text, with formatting, links, images, and a preview.
3. [Publish an entry and review its history](./publish-and-history.md) makes your work live and brings back an earlier version.
4. [When something goes wrong](./when-something-goes-wrong.md) helps you recover when a sign-in, a save, or a publish doesn't work.
5. [Add an image](./add-an-image.md) puts a picture in an entry and gives it a description.
6. [Manage the media library](./manage-the-media-library.md) finds, updates, and deletes the pictures your site holds.
7. [Manage your tags](./manage-your-tag-vocabulary.md) keeps the list of tags tidy so entries share the same ones.
