# Add an image

How to put a picture in the draft you're writing.

## Start the insert

With your cursor where you want the picture, either:

- Select **Insert image** in the toolbar, or
- Drag a picture file onto the writing surface, or paste one you've copied.

A small panel opens with **Upload an image** at the top. Below it, a search lets you
reuse a picture you've already uploaded to your site instead of adding a new one. Pick a
match from that list, and it's placed right away, no further steps.

## Uploading a new picture

Once you choose a file, either from your computer or by drag and drop, you'll see:

- A **Name** field. If your file already has a sensible name, it's filled in for you,
  marked **Suggested**. If it's something generic like a camera's file number, the field
  starts empty and you'll need to fill it in yourself.
- An **alt text** choice: write a short description, or mark the image decorative. Alt
  text is what a screen reader speaks in place of the picture, so it matters for anyone
  who can't see the image directly. See
  [Write in the editor](./write-in-the-editor.md#images) for how to decide between the
  two and what makes a good description.

You can select **Insert image** without alt text set, and add it later. The picture still
goes in either way; it just gets flagged as needing alt text until you fill it in.

For which file types the editor accepts, see
[Manage the media library](./manage-the-media-library.md#accepted-image-types).

## After it's in your draft

The picture appears right where your cursor was. From there, you can give it a caption
and choose how it sits on the page (Measure, Center, Wide, or Full), covered in
[Write in the editor](./write-in-the-editor.md#images).

If the upload doesn't go through, see
[When something goes wrong](./when-something-goes-wrong.md#an-image-wont-add).

## Setting an entry's lead picture

Open **Details** and set the entry's hero image to give the entry a lead picture, shown
at the top of the page. It's also what shows up when someone shares the entry on a
social site, so its own dialog previews the shape it gets cropped to there. It gets the
same alt-text-or-decorative choice and a caption as a picture in your text.
