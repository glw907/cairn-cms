# Manage your tag list

Tags are a shared list every editor on your site picks from when they write. This screen,
called **Tags**, is where that shared list is kept.

**Who can change it:** by default, any signed-in editor can. Your site can be set up to
limit it to specific people, though that takes a developer's help, not something your
site owner can do from here. If you don't see Tags in your sidebar at all, ask whoever
administers your site whether it's meant to be there for your account.

## Adding a tag

Type a name under **Add a tag**. As you type, a short stored form of it appears below the
field. This is the version other entries reference internally, and it doesn't change
even if you rename the tag later. Select **Add tag** to add it to the list.

A name needs at least one letter or number, and it can't duplicate a tag already on the
list. If either is true, the field tells you why and nothing is added.

## Renaming a tag

Edit the name directly in the list. This only changes the label editors see; every post
already carrying that tag keeps it correctly, since the stored form underneath doesn't
change.

## Removing a tag

Select the trash icon beside a tag with no posts using it, and it's removed right away.
A tag that's still in use shows how many posts carry it instead, and won't let you remove
it until you take it off those posts first.

## Tags already on your posts, but not on this list

If a tag shows up on your posts without being on this shared list (maybe it was added
before this list existed, or by another tool), it appears in its own section below the
main list with an **Add to list** button. Adding it here doesn't change anything about
the posts that already carry it: it just makes the tag choosable going forward.

## Saving

Nothing takes effect until you select **Save changes** at the bottom of the screen. Every
add, rename, and removal you've made is applied together, for the whole site, in one
step.
