## docs/editors/welcome.md
- Sign-in is by email address and a one-time link, no password or account. Source: `src/lib/components/LoginPage.svelte`; `src/lib/auth-channel/factory.ts`. [verified]
- The sign-in link expires in 10 minutes. Source: `src/lib/auth/crypto.ts:63` ("Magic-link tokens live 10 minutes"), `src/lib/email.ts:64` ("The link expires in 10 minutes"). [verified]
- The auth-channel factory clamps a configurable code lifetime to a default of 10 minutes and a max of 15 minutes, so a site can shorten or lengthen this within that ceiling. Source: `src/lib/auth-channel/factory.ts:325-326` (`code.ttlMs`, "Default 10 minutes, clamped to at most 15 minutes"). [verified]
- A confirm step after opening the email is a deliberate extra step so opening the email alone cannot sign a user in. Source: `src/lib/sveltekit/auth-routes.ts` (confirm route pattern); page text matches the documented email-scanner-safety rationale in `src/lib/auth-channel/factory.ts` design comments. [verified]
- The same "check your email" message shows whether or not the address is registered, to avoid leaking which addresses are real. Source: `src/lib/sveltekit/auth-routes.ts` (send-link handler; constant-response behavior for unknown vs known email). [verified]
- Requesting a sign-in link twice in quick succession does not send a second link; the requester is told to check their inbox or wait a minute. Source: `src/lib/components/LoginPage.svelte`; `src/lib/auth-channel/factory.ts` rate-limit/cooldown fields; exact string in when-something-goes-wrong.md matches an auth-routes response. [verified]
- A signed-in session lasts about a month (30 days). Source: `src/lib/auth/crypto.ts:66-67` (`SESSION_TTL_MS = 30 * 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000`, "Sessions live 30 days"). [verified]
- A site can configure the session lifetime up to a 1-year clamp, so "about a month" is the default, not a hard limit. Source: `src/lib/auth-channel/factory.ts:346` ("Session lifetime in ms. Default 30 days, clamped to at most 1 year"). [verified]
- The sign-out control lives with the editor's name in the sidebar. Source: `src/lib/components/CairnAdminShell.svelte:1137-1156` (the sidebar footer's name/email/role block, then the `POST /admin?/logout` "Sign out" button in the same footer). [verified]
- An editor whose account has no concepts assigned sees a plain welcome message with nothing to write in. Source: `src/lib/components/CairnAdmin.svelte:117-118` (`data.view === 'welcome'` renders `WelcomeView`); `src/lib/components/WelcomeView.svelte` (an `EmptyState` with "Welcome, {displayName}" and "There's no content here for this account to manage", no action). [verified]
