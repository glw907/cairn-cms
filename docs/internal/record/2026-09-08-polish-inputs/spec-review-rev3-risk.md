**3. Spec 409-411 and 212-214 -- "two concurrent full gates is the machine ceiling" is the wrong constraint; the binding one is a hardcoded port, and it binds inside polish-A alone.** `examples/showcase/playwright.config.ts:30-32,36` pins `npm run preview -- --port 4173`, `port: 4173`, and `baseURL: 'http://localhost:4173'`, with `reuseExistingServer: !process.env.CI`. Polish-A's per-task gate (spec 216-223) ends with "`CI=1` showcase e2e with every baseline unchanged", and polish-A is also told to run its independent block through the workflow's parallel mode (spec 212-214, 437-439), which `pass-execute-chains` runs in separate worktrees. Two chains in that mode each reach the e2e step: with `CI=1` set, `reuseExistingServer` is false and the second run cannot bind 4173, so the gate fails for a reason no task caused; with `CI` unset it silently attaches to the other worktree's preview server and proves the wrong tree. The same collision applies to any polish-A and polish-C overlap, so "two concurrent full gates" is not achievable at all while both gates carry the e2e. Separately, polish-A's e2e line lacks the qualifier polish-C's carries: polish-C says "the from-scratch showcase e2e" (spec 372-375) and polish-A does not, yet `CLAUDE.md`'s durable gotcha is explicit that a worktree's `examples/showcase/node_modules` symlinks back to the main checkout and resolves both `file:` deps to MAIN's build until a from-scratch `npm install` in the worktree's showcase repoints them. ## Sizing verdict

Split polish-A. Sixteen nominal tasks are honestly nineteen or twenty once Task 1 becomes three (finding 6) and the friction-log triage leaves Task 16 (finding 11), and this repo's measured band of 370K to 530K per task over five comparable passes funds thirteen to sixteen tasks at 7M on a lighter gate than this one; six of polish-A's tasks are each over 0.7M on their own deliverable counts. Cut by track, not by position: **polish-A1 (slice 11a), the engine code**, carries Tasks 1, 2, 13, 14, and 15 -- the two monolith splits with the `Reload` sweep as its own task after them, the Svelte lint wiring, the JSDoc residuals, and the two small engine items -- roughly eight tasks after the Task 1 split, at 4M, gated on `check:surface` byte-identity and the CLI suite, appending one CHANGELOG bullet and nothing else. **Polish-A2 (slice 11b), the admin surface**, carries Tasks 3 through 12 plus the records work -- the keyboard and combobox tasks, the design-system busy recipe ahead of the convergence, the live regions, the chip and SSR work, the two conformance halves, the `cairnAccess` seam, the signups exemplar, the friction log and ROADMAP as a task of their own, and the records task last -- twelve tasks at 5.5M, owning `ROADMAP.md`, the friction log, `docs/HISTORY.md`, and the render proofs, with the a11y, svelte, and security fan-out at its end. The cut point is clean because the two halves touch disjoint file sets (`src/lib/sveltekit/*` and `src/lib/components/*` plus `examples/showcase/src/routes/admin/*`), because it matches the sweeps that produced them (`exports-sweep.md` and `admin-sweep.md`), and because polish-C's anchor reconciliation then runs once against post-11a `main` where its renamed symbols actually live. Polish-C stays at ten tasks and 7M, which is right for its shape, provided finding 1 is resolved and its Task 1 is either moved to 2a or ordered ahead of 2a's branch.
