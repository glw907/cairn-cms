<p class="lead">The Trailhead Club has maintained the ridge and valley trail network since 1987, entirely with volunteer labor and member dues. This page covers who we are, how the club runs, and how to get involved.</p>

## How we started

A handful of hikers who had grown tired of watching the old fire-tower trail wash out every spring formed the club after a particularly bad storm in 1987 took out the bridge at mile three for the second time in five years. The founding group's first project was that bridge, and it is still standing.

### A note on the name

We are named for the stone trail markers, not for any particular trail. Every marked route in the network gets its own cairns, rebuilt by the work crew each spring.

> A trail that nobody maintains is just a rumor. Somebody has to keep it a fact.

## What the club does

- Maintains roughly forty miles of marked trail across the ridge and valley network
- Runs monthly group hikes, open to non-members
- Publishes trail condition reports after every organized outing
- Operates a small loaner closet of tents, stoves, and rain shells for new members

### Trail work, in order of how it usually goes

1. Scout the section
   1. Walk it end to end
   2. Log washouts, blowdowns, and any new hazards
2. Plan the crew
   - Assign a lead
   - Post the work date to the calendar
3. Do the work
   1. Clear blowdowns first
   2. Rebuild drainage
   3. Repaint blazes last, once the tread is fixed

## Getting involved

- [ ] Come to a monthly hike, no gear required beyond sturdy shoes
- [ ] Join a work day; tools and instruction are provided
- [ ] Consider sustaining membership once you know you'll stick around

Web links go to outside addresses, like the [regional trails coalition](https://example.com/trails-coalition) we coordinate with on interstate routes. Links to our own pages, like the [volunteer page](cairn:pages/minimal), keep working even if we later rename the page.

For questions the [volunteer page](cairn:pages/minimal) does not answer, email the committee directly.

## Contact and meeting times

The committee meets the first Tuesday of every month at the library annex. Members are welcome; bring trail condition reports if you have any.

`trailhead-committee@example.com` is the general inbox, and it is checked daily during the season.
