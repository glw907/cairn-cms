---
title: Mud season starts on the lower switchbacks
date: 2025-03-08
description: The lower switchbacks have gone soft with the thaw, and staying on the durable line matters more than usual this week.
topics:
  - trail-reports

---
The switchbacks below the saddle have gone from packed dirt to standing mud in the space of about a week, which is what happens once the frost comes out of the ground faster than the trail can drain it. Boot prints from the last few days have widened the soft sections by a foot or more on either side, the usual result of people stepping around a puddle instead of through it.

Stepping through the mud instead of skirting it keeps the trail narrow and lets it heal once the ground firms up in a few weeks. It also happens to be less work: the packed line under the water is often more solid footing than the loose dirt at the edge, which just gives way under your weight anyway.

Gaiters earn their keep this time of year more than any other. A low pair kept our socks dry through six or seven soft crossings that would otherwise have soaked through by the second mile.

The upper switchbacks, above where the trail gets more sun, were still firm and dry. If you only have time for part of the loop, that stretch is the better bet until the lower section drains out properly, probably another few weeks at this rate.
