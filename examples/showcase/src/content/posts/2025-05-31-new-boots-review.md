---
title: Breaking in a new pair of boots the slow way
date: 2025-05-31
description: A pair of stiff leather boots earned their fit over six short walks before the first real day on trail.
topics:
  - gear
---
A stiff leather boot straight out of the box is not something to trust on a long day, and this pair needed six short walks before it felt like part of my foot rather than a box around it. The first two walks were under three miles each, just around the neighborhood, enough to flex the leather at the ball of the foot without putting real mileage on a fit I hadn't tested yet.

By the fourth walk, a five-mile loop with some elevation, one hot spot showed up on the outside of my left heel. A strip of tape there for the next two walks solved it completely, and by the sixth walk the spot never got warm at all. That's usually the sign a boot is ready for something longer.

The insole that came with the boot was thinner than what I'm used to, so I swapped in a pair with more arch support before the break-in walks even started. Doing that early meant the leather molded around the actual insole I'd be using, not a stand-in that would change the fit later.

Eight miles this week with a full pack and no hot spots anywhere. Worth the two weeks it took to get here.
