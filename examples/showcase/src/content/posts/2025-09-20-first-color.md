title: First color on the aspen bench
date: 2025-09-20
description: The aspen bench turned gold almost overnight this week, and the cooler mornings are worth planning around.

---
The aspen stand on the upper bench went from green to gold in what felt like four or five days, faster than the slow fade we usually see there. A hard frost two nights ago likely pushed the change along all at once rather than the gradual turn the bench usually gets.

Mornings have dropped into the thirties this week even as afternoons still climb into the sixties, which makes for a wide temperature swing over a single hike. A layer that felt necessary at the trailhead was tied around a pack by ten in the morning and back on again once the sun dropped behind the ridge on the way down.

The bench itself gets busy fast once the color peaks, and the small trailhead lot was already full by nine on a weekday. Weekday mornings before the color fully turns are the better bet for anyone who wants the bench without a crowd; by the weekend, word travels and the lot fills before eight.

The gold won't hold long at this pace. A hard wind or a heavier frost in the next week or two will likely bring most of it down, so this is close to the peak window for the bench.
