---
title: Invite an editor
date: 2026-11-03
description: Add a person to the list of people who can sign in and write on your site.
author: author
---
Only the site's owner can add people. You'll need the person's name and the email address they'll sign in with.

1. Go to `/admin/editors`.
2. At the bottom of the screen, enter the person's name and email address.
3. Choose their role. The default is **editor**, who can write and publish. An **owner** can also add and remove people.
4. Select **Add editor**.

The screen sends no email. The person signs themselves in when they're ready: they go to your site's `/admin`, enter their email address, and receive a sign-in link.

Sign-in email needs a domain connected to your site. Until you connect one, you're the only person who can sign in, and adding someone changes nothing for them.

To remove someone or change their role, go back to the same screen and use the buttons on their row.
