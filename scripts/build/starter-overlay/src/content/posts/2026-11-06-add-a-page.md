A page holds writing that doesn't belong in a dated list, such as an About page or a list of services. A page has an address on your site and no date, and it stays out of the archive.

To add a page, follow these steps:

1. In the sidebar, select **Pages**, and then select **New page**.
2. In the **Title** box, type the page's title, such as `Services`.
3. Check the **Address** box. A page titled `Services` gets the address `services`, so the page lives at `/services`.
4. Select **Create**, write the page, and then select **Publish**.

A page doesn't appear in your site's menu automatically. To send readers to it, link to it from a post or another page, as [Link to another page](cairn:posts/2026-11-04-link-to-another-page) explains. To change the menu itself, edit the `menus` list in the site's `src/site/site.config.yaml` file, or ask the person who maintains your site's code.
