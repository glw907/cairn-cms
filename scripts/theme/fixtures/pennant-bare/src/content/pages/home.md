## What we do

::::cards{columns="3"}
:::items
- [Map night](/programs) *Members and visitors.* We spread a sheet across the tables and read it together, from the contour lines to the place names. *Second Tuesday of each month, 7 p.m.*
- [Field days](/programs) *Members of every age.* We take a sheet outside with map and compass and find its points on the ground. *One Saturday a month, June through August.*
- [Junior program](/programs) *Ages 10 to 16.* Young members learn to read a map and take a bearing, with two adults at every session. *Map nights all year, field days in summer.*
:::
::::

## Where and when

::::cards{columns="3"}
:::items
- **Where** Kittiwake Hall, Spenard, Anchorage.
- **When** The second Tuesday of each month, at 7 p.m.
- **Questions** Write to info@turnagain-geographic.example, and a volunteer will answer within a week.
:::
