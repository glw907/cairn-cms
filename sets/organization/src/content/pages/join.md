Anyone can join the Turnagain Geographic Society. We have about 140 members, from students to retired surveyors, and you don't need any experience with maps.

## How to join

The easiest way is to come to a map night. We meet at Kittiwake Hall in Spenard on the second Tuesday of each month at 7 p.m., and your first map night is free. Tell a volunteer at the door that you'd like to join, and they'll give you a membership form.

If you can't come in person, write to info@turnagain-geographic.example, and a volunteer will send you the form within a week.

## What members get

Members come to every map night and field day, borrow sheets from the lending shelf, and get the field-day plan by email before each one. A member can also sign up a young person for the junior program.

## Young members

Young people aged 10 to 16 join through the junior program, with a parent's or guardian's consent. [Programs](/programs) describes what they do, and [Safeguarding and privacy](/safeguarding) explains how we keep them safe.
