The society runs four programs: a monthly map night, summer field days, a junior program for ages 10 to 16, and a lending shelf of topographic sheets. Members take part in all of them, from students to retired surveyors.

## Map night

Map night is the second Tuesday of each month at 7 p.m., at Kittiwake Hall in Spenard. One member brings a sheet, and the room reads it together: the contour lines, the place names, and the survey notes in the margin. Visitors are welcome, and your first map night is free.

## Field days

From June through August, the society spends one Saturday a month outside with map and compass. We choose a sheet, mark a few points on it, and find them on the ground. The field-day lead sends members the plan and the meeting place a week ahead.

Our sheets cover the places the society has studied since 1974: Turnagain Arm, Knik Arm, the Chugach Mountains, the Kenai Peninsula, and Portage Valley.

## Junior program

The junior program is for members aged 10 to 16. Young members learn to orient a map, take a bearing, and read contour lines, first at map night and then on field days. Two adults are present at every session. [Safeguarding and privacy](/safeguarding) explains how the program is run.

## Lending shelf

The society keeps a shelf of topographic sheets at the hall. Members can borrow a sheet for a month, and the shelf is open at every map night. Most of the sheets are USGS maps, and some are older than the society.
