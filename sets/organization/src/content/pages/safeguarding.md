The society runs a junior program for members aged 10 to 16. This page says how we keep those members safe, and how we handle every member's details.

## The junior program

Two adults are present at every junior session, at map night and on field days, so no adult is ever alone with a young member. Before each field day, the junior program's coordinator tells parents the plan: where the group will go, when it leaves and returns, and how to reach the adults in charge. A parent or guardian signs a consent form when a young member joins.

## Photos of young members

We publish a photo of a young member only with a parent's written consent. We never put a young member's full name beside a photo of them, on this site or anywhere else the society publishes.

## Members' details

We keep each member's name and mail address, and for a young member, a parent's contact details. We use them only to run the society: to send the plans for map nights and field days, and to reach a parent during a junior session. We never sell them or share them with anyone.

To have your details deleted, write to info@turnagain-geographic.example. A volunteer will delete them and write back to confirm.

## Questions

Write to the junior program's coordinator at juniors@turnagain-geographic.example about the junior program, or to info@turnagain-geographic.example about anything else.
